\subsection{Propagation of the Local Cutoff}
\label{app:local-propagation}

For \(0\le r,a\le1\), write
\[
 M_{\rm loc}(r,a)=(1-r^2)h(ar)-(1-ar^2)h(r).
\]
Yu's threshold-propagation lemma~\cite[Lemma~4.9]{Yu}, in sign means,
states that \(M_{\rm loc}(r_1,a)\ge0\), with \(0<r_1<1\), implies
\(M_{\rm loc}(r,a)\ge0\) for \(0\le r\le r_1\).
Since \(M_{\rm loc}(r,\cdot)\) is concave and
\(M_{\rm loc}(r,1)=0\), a check at \((r_1,a_0)\), with
\(a_0\in[0,1]\), therefore covers
all \(0\le r\le r_1\) and \(a_0\le a\le1\).
